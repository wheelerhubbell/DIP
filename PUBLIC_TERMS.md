# WHP DIP Public Terms v1

Version identifier: `WHP-DIP-PUBLIC-TERMS-v1`

These terms describe the public Decision Integrity Protocol interface and verification materials published by Wheeler Hubbell Publishing, Inc. They do not publish or grant possession of the private evaluator, signing private key, production runtime, payment controls, or private operational materials.

## Public use

The public protocol description, schemas, examples, release identity, and verification materials may be inspected and used to understand the DIP interface, construct compatible requests, parse returned results, and verify whether a presented result satisfies WHP's published official-result verification requirements.

## Official results

A result is not official merely because it follows a public schema, reproduces a public example, names DIP, or implements similar logic. An official WHP DIP result must satisfy `OFFICIAL_RESULT_VERIFICATION.md`, including a valid signature under an active WHP key published in the public key registry for the issuance time.

## Independent implementations

A third party may describe its software as compatible with the published DIP interface only if that statement is accurate. Compatibility, reproduction, or independent implementation does not make a third-party result an official WHP-issued result and does not grant WHP institutional authority, a WHP Standing Mark, or authority to impersonate WHP.

## No authority creation

Calling, implementing, or verifying DIP does not create legal authority, organizational authority, jurisdiction, a warrant, a payment right, or permission that did not otherwise exist. DIP evaluates asserted authority within a bounded request; it does not manufacture authority.

## No certification claim

DIP output is not, merely by being a DIP output, legal advice, legal certification, safety certification, security certification, professional certification, a universal truth verdict, or a WHP Standing Mark.

## Private boundary

Nothing in this public repository represents the private evaluator implementation, canonical executable runtime, private signing material, production credentials, payment secrets, wallet controls, private tests, or future private implementation improvements as public assets.

These public terms may be versioned prospectively. An official result identifies the terms version applicable to that issuance.
